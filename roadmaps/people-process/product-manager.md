# Product Manager

## One-liner

The role that decides *what* to build and *why* — owns the product vision, prioritization, and outcome metrics; bridges business, design, and engineering.

## What It Is

A [Product Manager (PM)](https://roadmap.sh/product-manager) decides what the team builds, in what order, and why. They own the product vision, set the strategy, prioritize the roadmap, define success metrics, and align stakeholders. In 2026, PMs often specialize: growth PM, platform PM, AI PM, etc.

Tools: [ProductPlan](https://www.productplan.com/), [Mind the Product](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/), Linear, Jira, Notion, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Pendo, PostHog.

Adoption: Every product team has a PM (or founder wearing the hat). PM is the #1 non-engineering role in tech.

## When To Use It

- **You lead product strategy** — PM.
- **You're a founder** — PM hat.
- **You want outcome-driven roadmaps** — PM frameworks.

## When NOT To Use It

- **Tiny project** — founders do it.
- **Internal tools** — eng team drives.

## Why It Matters in 2026

Three forces: (1) AI products need new PM thinking; (2) Continuous discovery is the norm; (3) Outcome-based roadmaps over output-based.

Practitioner playbook: (1) Weekly user touchpoints; (2) Outcome metrics (not output); (3) JTBD framing; (4) Prioritize ruthlessly; (5) Communicate trade-offs; (6) Test with prototypes.
