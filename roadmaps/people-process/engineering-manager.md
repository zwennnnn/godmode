# Engineering Manager

## One-liner

The role that leads an engineering team — combines technical judgment with people management (hiring, coaching, performance, planning); the bridge between ICs and senior leadership.

## What It Is

An [Engineering Manager (EM)](https://roadmap.sh/engineering-manager) leads a team of engineers: hiring, coaching, performance reviews, project planning, technical direction, cross-team coordination, and team health. In 2026, the role splits into specializations: people manager (focus on team), tech lead (focus on architecture), and the staff+ IC track (no reports).

Adoption: Every engineering team >5 has an EM. Staff+ ICs are an alternative track.

## When To Use It

- **You lead engineers** — EM.
- **You want to grow from senior IC to manager** — EM role.
- **You're a tech lead who wants management** — pivot to EM.

## When NOT To Use It

- **You hate people work** — staff IC track.
- **Tiny team** — founder does it.

## Why It Matters in 2026

Three forces: (1) Remote + hybrid work — managers must lead async; (2) AI tools change what engineering teams do — managers re-scope work; (3) Staff+ IC tracks let senior engineers skip management.

Practitioner playbook: (1) 1:1s are sacred — weekly, no agenda, take notes; (2) Hire slowly, fire fast; (3) Coach via questions; (4) Outcome-based goals; (5) Protect the team from chaos; (6) Read Will Larson.

## Comparison With Alternatives

| Alternative | Better when | Worse when |
|-------------|-------------|------------|
| **Tech Lead (no reports)** | You love code; hate HR. | You want broad impact. |
| **Staff Engineer (no reports)** | Senior IC track. | You want to manage. |
| **Founder-led team** | Early startup. | Series A+. |
| **EM-as-tech-lead** | Small team. | Larger orgs. |
